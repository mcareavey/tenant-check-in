How this form works

  1. Go through your room and the shared areas on the day you move in, ideally with whoever is checking you in.
  2. Tick one box per item. Good: clean, no marks or damage. Fair: minor wear or small marks. Poor: damaged, stained, broken or not working. N/A: not there. Anything marked Fair or Poor needs a note saying what's wrong and where. Each area has an "All Good" button: mark anything that isn't Good first, then press it to fill in the rest.
  3. Add clear photos of your room and of anything you've noted, with a few words on what each one shows.
  4. Sign at the end and press Send.
  5. If you find anything else within 7 days of moving in, email your landlord a description and photos. Those emails become part of this record.
  6. At check-out the room and shared areas are compared with this record, allowing for fair wear and tear.

Your answers are saved on this device as you go, so you can stop and come back. They're cleared once the form has been sent. Photos aren't saved until you press Send.
